# Screenshot analysis

All eight source screenshots were inspected before implementation. Repeated headers on slides 1–2 are treated as one sticky header; screenshots are section references, not eight flattened background images.

1. **Hero:** warm off-white ground, blue compass brand, six-item navigation, rounded inquiry button. Large extra-bold royal-blue headline at left, supporting copy, blue/outlined actions, three color dots. Rounded artwork at right shows the explorer mascot and condominium setting. Original artwork is extracted from slide 1.
2. **Properties:** centered two-line heading and explanation, pill filters, three equal rounded white cards. Quezon City tags, neutral image placeholders, soft lower gradient, bedroom notice and paired actions. Projects: Erin Heights, The Oriana, The Calinea.
3. **Family fit:** asymmetrical two-column section. Orange tracked eyebrow, blue headline, two layout note cards and orange CTA at left. Four white life-stage cards in a 2×2 grid at right, pale-cyan icon tiles.
4. **Tools:** centered heading, four white cards with cyan icon tiles. Muted dashed card and orange coming-soon tag for 3D tours. Outlined CTA below with tiny disclosure.
5. **Benefits:** full-width deep royal-blue ground, white headline and copy. Three-column, two-row translucent blue cards. White icon tiles; lower-right card is orange with a white pill CTA.
6. **Journal:** left-aligned orange eyebrow and two-line headline, right-aligned browse link, three rounded editorial cards. Neutral photo placeholders with category tags, blue titles, grey-blue summaries and orange read links.
7. **Contact:** left introduction, two contact option cards and pale-cyan privacy note. Right rounded white form with paired fields, warm-grey inputs, consent and full-width blue action.
8. **Footer:** deep navy, three columns, compact brand and social controls, navigation and contact details, divider, small disclosures, copyright and policy controls.

Approximate palette: royal blue #0036a3; warm background #f3f0eb; orange #ff8e39; cyan #d2eff9; footer #111d35. Poppins headings and Open Sans body closely match the screenshot shapes; original font files/specifications were not supplied. Cards use 22px radii, fine cool-grey borders and subtle shadows. Section widths are capped at 1280px with flexible gutters; mobile stacks preserve section order and visual hierarchy.

Intentional truthfulness changes: missing inventory is not inferred, incomplete articles open labeled previews, unsupported tools disclose missing assets, unverified contacts are not live links, and the form validates locally rather than claiming delivery/database storage.
